# Testing Session Script — Financial Brain v0.1

## Before You Start

### Do NOT say:

- "This is an AI finance app I'm building"
- "I want to validate my startup idea"
- "Tell me if you'd use this"

### Instead say:

> "I'm testing a financial planning tool. I want to know if it actually helps people make better money decisions. I'm not testing you — I'm testing the tool."

---

## Setup

1. Share the link: `https://your-deployment.vercel.app`
2. Ask them to open it on their phone or laptop
3. Do NOT explain how to use it
4. Watch silently for 5 minutes

---

## Step 1 — Silent Exploration (5 minutes)

Observe and take notes:

| What to watch | Notes |
|-------------|-------|
| Where do they click first? | |
| Do they read the scenarios? | |
| Do they edit the numbers? | |
| Where do they hesitate? | |
| What confuses them? | |
| Do they scroll past something important? | |
| Do they try to click something that doesn't exist? | |

**Do not interrupt.** Let them figure it out.

If they ask a question about how to use it, say:

> "Try what feels natural. There's no wrong way."

---

## Step 2 — Scenario A: Broke Student (5 minutes)

Say:

> "Click 'Broke Student' at the top, then 'Analyze My Situation'. Tell me what you think."

Observe:

- Do they read the analysis?
- Do they scroll through all sections?
- Do they nod or frown?
- Any facial reactions?

Then ask:

> "Does this advice feel realistic for someone in that situation?"

---

## Step 3 — Scenario B: Their Own Situation (5 minutes)

Say:

> "Now put in your own situation. Use real numbers if you're comfortable, or estimate."

Let them type freely.

Observe:

- What fields do they fill first?
- What do they leave blank?
- Do they add income sources? Commitments? Goals?

After they analyze, ask:

> "What part felt most useful?"

---

## Step 4 — Feedback Form (2 minutes)

Say:

> "There's a feedback form on the analysis page. Click 'Give Feedback'. Be honest — it helps me know what to fix."

Watch them fill it out.

If they skip a question, don't prompt them.

---

## Step 5 — Debrief Questions (5 minutes)

After they submit feedback, ask these verbally:

### Question 1

> "What advice felt wrong or off?"

(This is the most important question. Listen carefully.)

### Question 2

> "When in your real life would you use this?"

Possible answers to listen for:

- "When I get paid"
- "When I'm broke and don't know what to do"
- "Every Sunday before church"
- "When I want to know if I can afford something"
- "Probably never"

### Question 3

> "What financial problem does this solve for you?"

If they say "I don't know" → the value isn't clear yet.

If they say "It helps me know what I can safely spend" → strong signal.

### Question 4

> "What's one thing you wish it did?"

---

## What NOT to Ask

❌ "Would you download this?" (measures politeness)
❌ "Do you like it?" (measures politeness)
❌ "Is this a good idea?" (measures politeness)

---

## After the Session

### Same-day notes

Write down within 1 hour:

1. What did they say that surprised you?
2. What did they struggle with?
3. Did they trust the output?
4. Would they use it again?
5. Quote anything memorable they said

### Tag the session

- User type: Student / Freelancer / Salaried / Business
- Income type: Regular / Irregular / Mixed
- Overall reaction: Positive / Neutral / Negative
- Would use weekly: Yes / Maybe / No

## Troubleshooting

### If they say "I don't understand"

Say: "Just put in some numbers and see what happens. You can't break it."

### If they say "The numbers seem wrong"

Say: "That's useful feedback. What would you expect instead?"

### If they ask about OpenAI API key

Say: "That's for an enhanced version. The basic analysis works without it."

### If they spend more than 2 minutes on the input form

Say: "Try the 'Broke Student' scenario at the top to see it work first."
